Email pricing builder

Competitors discount by volume, so their price curve bends. A flat per-subscriber rate does not. This tool shows where the two cross, and what it costs on either side.

The pricing exponent

Every plan curve can be written as price ∝ subscriberse. A flat per-subscriber rate has e = 1: double the list, double the bill. Every competitor sits below 1, which is why they get relatively cheaper as lists grow.

On the chart below, log scales on both axes turn each curve into a straight line. The slope is the exponent. Ours is the steep one.
